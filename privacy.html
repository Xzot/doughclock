Dough Clock

What happens to your bread data

Effective November 2026. Dough Clock is made by Vlad Shchuka.

The short version: nothing comes to me. I run no servers and I can't see your formulas, your photos or how your bulk went.

What I collect

Nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party code that collects anything. The App Store privacy label says Data Not Collected.

On your devices

Your formulas, starters, feedings, bake book and settings are kept on your iPhone and iPad. The bake in progress and your settings sit in storage shared between the app and its widget (an App Group).

In your iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, Dough Clock keeps your devices in step using your own private iCloud database and iCloud key-value storage: your bake book and its photos, your starters and feedings, your own formulas, the bake in progress, your personal calibration, and your unit and stripe choices. That data is under your Apple Account, it counts toward your iCloud storage, and Apple's privacy policy covers it. I have no way to read it. To turn it off, go to Settings, your name, iCloud, and switch off Dough Clock. Dough Clock then works on that device alone.

Photos and camera

You can add photos of your bread and your starter jar. If you pick one from your library, the app receives only the photo you picked. If you take one, iOS asks for camera access first. The app keeps a smaller copy, and syncs it through your iCloud as described above.

Bells and the Lock Screen

Fold, step and feeding reminders are local notifications. Live Activities show the current step on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. Your device schedules and shows all of them. Nothing goes through me.

Sharing a formula

When you export a formula, the app hands the text or PDF to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next is up to you.

Paying

Dough Clock Pro is sold by Apple through the App Store. I never see your payment details. The app only asks Apple whether your Apple Account owns Pro.

Children

The app isn't aimed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
